STAY SAFE

A few habits worth keeping.

None of this is specific to Ark Hub — it's just good practice anywhere you're running third-party tools.

  • Use an alt when testing

    Keep new scripts on a secondary account until you trust them.

  • Never share your password

    No one legitimate on the team will ever ask for it — not for "support," not for anything.

  • Report anything that looks off

    Fake giveaways, DMs asking for info, or lookalike links — flag it in the Discord.
